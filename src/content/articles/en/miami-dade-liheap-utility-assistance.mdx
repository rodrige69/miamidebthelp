Miami-Dade County's **Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP)** is part of the County's Light Bill Assistance service. The County says LIHEAP helps qualified Miami-Dade residents with home energy bills and may provide additional assistance in an energy crisis. [1]

The County lists several eligibility and documentation requirements, including Miami-Dade residency, U.S. citizenship or legal permanent resident status, proof of recent household income, identification, and a current electricity bill with a final or past-due notice — or a disconnection. [1]

The County's current page directs applicants to an online registration option and identifies **786-469-4640** as the program phone number. For an electricity account that has been disconnected or is facing disconnection, the County directs residents to visit a Community Resource Center; it says appointments are encouraged and walk-ins are welcome. [1]

## What LIHEAP is in Miami-Dade

LIHEAP is a federal program. The U.S. Department of Health and Human Services says it helps families with energy costs, including home energy bills and energy crises. [2]

Miami-Dade County describes its local LIHEAP service as assistance for qualified residents' energy bills, with additional help in cases of an energy crisis. [1] The County places LIHEAP within its broader Light Bill Assistance service, which is designed to support low-income households struggling with electricity bills. [1]

> **Plain-English meaning:** LIHEAP is not a general cash grant. It is an energy-assistance program administered locally, and the County's program requirements and funding controls matter.

Federal HHS also states that LIHEAP does not provide direct grants to individuals and does not charge a fee for a benefit. [2] A request for payment in exchange for a LIHEAP benefit is not part of the federal program description.

## Who may qualify under Miami-Dade's current requirements

The County's LIHEAP page lists several threshold requirements and documents. They are not a guarantee of approval; they are the factors the County says it uses to review a household's program eligibility. [1]

| Requirement or record | What Miami-Dade lists |
|---|---|
| County connection | The applicant must reside in Miami-Dade County. [1] |
| Immigration / citizenship status | The County lists U.S. citizenship or legal permanent resident status. [1] |
| Adult identification | Current Florida driver's license or picture identification for household adults age 18 and older. [1] |
| Household income evidence | Proof of income for the past 30 days, including income information for household members. [1] |
| Electricity-bill evidence | A current electricity bill with a final or past-due notice from the provider, or evidence of disconnection. [1] |
| Additional records, when applicable | The County lists SNAP documentation, certain benefit statements, records concerning children age five or younger, and a Section 8 / public-housing utility-subsidy form where applicable. [1] |

The County's list is more detailed than a simple "low-income" label. It includes household identity, income, residence, and utility-bill documentation. It also says additional documents may be requested during application review when needed to determine eligibility or explain current income that appears insufficient to cover household expenses. [1]

This article does not convert those requirements into an individual eligibility decision. For example, the County's page requires income documentation but does not let a general article decide how the County will evaluate a particular household's income, expenses, or records.

## The two LIHEAP assistance categories described by the County

Miami-Dade's LIHEAP page distinguishes between **crisis energy assistance** and **regular home energy assistance.** [1]

### Crisis energy assistance

The County says its LIHEAP program is providing crisis energy assistance to qualified Miami-Dade residents experiencing hardship paying a home energy bill. The page states that eligible households can receive **up to $2,000 within a 12-month period.** [1]

A person whose electricity has been disconnected or who faces disconnection is in a different procedural position from a person seeking routine assistance. The County directs those residents to visit a Community Resource Center. It says appointments are encouraged and walk-ins are welcome. [1]

### Regular home energy assistance

The County says regular home energy assistance allows **one benefit payment once a year** for eligible households and does not require an emergency or crisis. [1]

The County states that the assistance amount depends on household income, household size, and household composition. It lists a **maximum of $1,350**, and expressly notes that the maximum is not the same as the amount of a household's current bill. [1]

| Category | County description |
|---|---|
| Crisis assistance | For qualified residents experiencing hardship with a home energy bill; up to $2,000 within 12 months. [1] |
| Regular assistance | One benefit payment per year for eligible households; an emergency is not required. [1] |
| Regular-assistance amount | Based on household income, size, and composition; stated maximum is $1,350. [1] |

These are current descriptions from the County's page, not a promise that any household will receive a particular amount or that funding will be available at the time of review.

## What documents the County lists

Gathering documents is a separate question from qualifying. The County's page lists records that help it review identity, household composition, income, utility status, and benefits. [1]

For income, the County gives examples that include current-year disability or SSI benefit statements, Social Security benefit statements, consecutive pay stubs, an employer letter verifying start date, pay rate, pay frequency, and average weekly hours, pension records, child-support verification, TANF verification, veteran-benefit information, workers' compensation benefits, and certain notarized assistance letters with supporting income documentation. [1]

The County also names a SNAP benefits printout, birth certificates for children age five or younger, and a copy of Form 50059 showing the current utility subsidy or allowance for households receiving Section 8 or public housing. [1]

> **Important distinction:** A document list tells applicants what the County may need to review. It does not show that a household has been approved, nor does it establish the amount of a benefit.

## How the official application process begins

Miami-Dade's Light Bill Assistance page provides an official online option to **register to submit an application**. It also lists the LIHEAP program number, **786-469-4640**. [1]

The County's emergency instruction is more specific for electricity disconnection or a pending disconnection: visit a Community Resource Center. According to the County page, appointments are encouraged and walk-ins are welcomed. [1]

For regular assistance, the County describes an approval-or-denial notice being sent by mail. It says that, when an application is approved, a payment credit will be sent to the utility provider within 45 days. [1]

The phrase "within 45 days" comes from the County's current program description. It should not be read as a prediction that every application will be approved or as a substitute for checking the County's current program status.

## Why an official source matters

LIHEAP is federally funded but locally administered. HHS describes the federal program's energy-cost and energy-crisis purpose, while Miami-Dade's own page provides the local requirements, assistance categories, document list, and application routes. [1] [2]

This distinction matters because local funding, program processing, and documentation requirements can change. For a current Miami-Dade application question, the County's Light Bill Assistance service is the source that controls local program instructions in this article. ## Frequently asked questions

### Does Miami-Dade LIHEAP help with electricity bills?

Yes. Miami-Dade describes its Light Bill Assistance program as support for low-income households struggling with electricity bills and says LIHEAP helps qualified residents pay energy bills. [1]

### Must a household live in Miami-Dade County?

Yes. The County lists Miami-Dade residency as an eligibility requirement. [1]

### What proof of income does the County request?

The County lists proof of income for the past 30 days and provides examples such as pay stubs, benefit statements, employer information, and other income records. [1]

### What happens if electricity is disconnected or a shutoff is pending?

The County says residents in that situation must visit a Community Resource Center. It says appointments are encouraged and walk-ins are welcome. [1]

### Can LIHEAP regular assistance be used without an emergency?

The County says an emergency or crisis is not required for its regular home energy assistance category. [1]

### Does LIHEAP charge an application or benefit fee?

No. HHS states that LIHEAP does not charge a fee for receiving a benefit. [2]
